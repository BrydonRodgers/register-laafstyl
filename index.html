Features

Everything your business needs

From FAQ replies to appointment booking, all on WhatsApp, with no extra app for your customers.

🤖

AI-powered replies

Set up with your business details. Answers questions about pricing, products and policies in your brand's tone.
📅

Appointment booking

Customers book services in the WhatsApp chat: service, date, time and confirmation. Included in Pack 2.
🎤

Voice note support

Voice messages are transcribed and answered, so voice enquiries don't go unanswered. Included in Pack 2.
📊

Personal dashboard

Every client gets their own dashboard with analytics, conversation history, an AI prompt editor, business hours and invoices.
🔔

Escalation alerts

When a customer asks for a human, you're notified so you can step in.
🧾

Automatic invoicing

Monthly invoices are generated for you, with paid and unpaid tracking.
Languages

Pick the language your assistant answers in

At sign-up you choose from all 11 official South African languages, or leave it on multilingual auto-detect. Test it in your own language during the free trial.

How It Works

How you get started

No coding needed. You register, we set up your assistant, and you link your WhatsApp by scanning one QR code.

1

Register your business

Fill in your details below. We create your account.
2

We configure your AI

Our team loads your business info, services, FAQs, and preferred language into your custom AI assistant.
3

Scan one QR code

Link your WhatsApp number by scanning a single QR code. Your assistant then starts answering on that number.
4

Manage from your dashboard

Edit your AI, review conversations, update business hours and view invoices from your personal client dashboard.
